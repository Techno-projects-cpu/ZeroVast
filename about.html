About

One developer.
No hand-off.

I'm Nezaad. I design, build and maintain websites from Khammam, Telangana — and you work with me directly, from the first call to the deploy.

I build websites. It is the whole business, and that is deliberate.

Most small businesses do not need a bigger website. They need a faster one — one that loads on a five-year-old Android phone over a patchy connection, and one they can update without calling a developer every time a sentence changes.

I started writing code for exactly that reason, and it has stayed the same ever since. Since then I have shipped marketing sites, internal tools and performance rescues for people in and around Khammam, always under the same rule: measure first, build second, and never ship anything I cannot explain line by line.

NEZAAD Industries is the business side of it. The work itself is still one person reading the code, writing the code, and picking up the message when something breaks.

01 / Readable codeWrite for the next person.

Including future me, on a bad day, with no context. If a file needs a comment to explain what it does, it needs a rewrite instead.

02 / Performance is a featureSpeed is part of the brief.

A page that takes three seconds to appear has already lost the visitor. Budgets for LCP, CLS and INP are agreed before the first commit.

03 / Plain communicationStraight answers, quickly.

Fixed scope, honest timelines, and a clear no when something is not worth building. The phone call is how I work.
