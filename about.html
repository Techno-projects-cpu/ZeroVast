The story so far

Start small.
Think enormous.

A young venture from rural India, built on resourcefulness—and pointed firmly at a much bigger future.

NEZAAD Industries began with a simple belief: limited resources should never limit the size of an idea.

Founded by a young creator from rural India, NEZAAD is taking on digital work with curiosity, discipline and as much frugality as possible.

Today, we create websites, AI-generated videos and image edits. Every project helps us sharpen our craft, serve real people and work toward better tools—including a capable computer that can take our ideas further.

This is only the beginning. We are building useful things now so we can attempt extraordinary things in the future.
